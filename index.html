Cornote

Turns any PDF into Cornell notes, practice questions and a study plan that adapts every time a student practises.

  • Four autonomous Claude agents: feedback, study plan, verifier and coach
  • Own tool-calling loop on the Anthropic SDK, no agent framework
  • Seven question types, spaced repetition and a timed exam mode
  • 237 tests passing; deployed on AWS Lightsail

ChamaHub

Management for chamas, Kenya's savings groups: contributions, loans, repayments and withdrawals in one shared ledger.

  • M-Pesa payments through Payhero, confirmed by webhook
  • Tamper-evident audit trail recorded on the Stellar blockchain
  • Member and treasurer dashboards with a loan approval workflow
  • Encrypted key storage and an audit log of sensitive actions
